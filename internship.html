Internship Program

Gain hands-on experience in AI operating systems and neural link research

Opportunities

Internship Tracks

Choose the path that aligns with your career goals

Research Internship

Work alongside our research team on cutting-edge neural link and AI operating system projects. Contribute to academic publications and open-source development.

  • Duration: 3-6 months
  • Remote or on-site
  • Academic credit available

Engineering Internship

Develop real-world embedded systems, contribute to our open-source projects, and gain practical engineering experience.

  • Duration: 3-6 months
  • Remote or on-site
  • Mentorship included

Technical Writing

Help document our research, create tutorials, and develop educational content for the embedded systems community.

  • Duration: 3-6 months
  • Fully remote
  • Flexible schedule

  • Review and feedback on drafts
  • Presentation preparation
