EmbeddedOS Foundation · 2026

An open ecosystem.
Foundation-governed. Industry-deployed.

The EmbeddedOS Research Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit governing the development of fourteen open-source embedded AI components — but the ecosystem extends well beyond the code: working groups, industry partners, regional chapters, contributors, and sister projects across the broader open source community.

Working Groups

Where the foundation does its real work

Six active groups drive the technical and editorial direction of EoS — open RFCs, member-only briefings, public votes.

IEC 61508 SIL-3

Safety-Certified

Driving the EoS-S subset toward SIL-3 evidence packages, in collaboration with two industry members. Quarterly reviews, public RFCs.

Join the working group →
On-device guidelines

Embedded AI Ethics

Publishing guidelines for on-device model usage — data minimization, on-device-only flags, opt-out attestation patterns.

Join the working group →
BCI · open data

Neural Interface Standards

Coordinating with academic groups on a reference electrode-array interface and an open dataset license.

Join the working group →
Bit-identical

Reproducible Builds

Maintaining the foundation's continuously-rebuilt reference images. Every release artifact is bit-identical and signed.

Join the working group →
i18n + a11y

Documentation & Localization

Coordinating documentation translation (en/de/ja/zh/hi) and accessibility audits across all foundation surfaces.

Open hardware reference designs for popular MCU/SoC platforms with full schematics, BOMs, and PCB layouts.

Governance

Member-governed. Open by default.

The EoS Research Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. The Board is elected by sustaining members. Every governance vote is published within 48 hours with full member-by-member breakdown. Working groups operate by lazy consensus with public RFCs.
