What Your Donation Funds

Every contribution goes directly to advancing open-source embedded systems research.

🧠

EoS Kernel Development

Funding the core real-time OS kernel, scheduler, memory manager, and hardware abstraction layers for ARM, RISC-V, and beyond.

🤖

AI & Neural Interface Research

Advancing eAI edge inference and eNI neural interface technology — including the 1,024-channel sEMG pipeline and BCI research.

📚

Open Documentation

Keeping all 14 technical books, API references, and tutorials free and publicly available for every developer worldwide.

🎓

Education & Internships

Supporting student internships, certification programs, and the EmbeddedOS Kids Edition for the next generation of engineers.

🔧

Hardware Lab & Testing

Maintaining compatibility with 52+ boards across 7 chip families — STM32, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, NXP, Nordic, TI, and more.

✈

Aerospace Research

Developing AeroSwift — a DO-178C-compliant VTOL aircraft platform powered by EoS, advancing autonomous flight safety.
