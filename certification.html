How to Earn Your Certification

All certifications are free to attempt. As a 501(c)(3) non-profit, we reinvest all program fees into open-source research and community education.

1
Choose Your Track
Select the certification that matches your career goals and current experience level.
2
Study the Curriculum
Access free study materials, open-source projects, and community resources on our GitHub.
3
Apply Online
Submit your application via email. No upfront fees — we believe in accessible education.
4
Complete Assessment
Take the online exam and/or submit a practical project demonstrating your skills.
5
Earn Your Badge
Receive your digital credential and printable certificate. Share on LinkedIn and GitHub.
