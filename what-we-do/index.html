What We Build

Five product families that compose into any embedded system — from a 32 KB microcontroller to a quad-core application processor.

EoS + eBoot
OS Foundation

Real-time kernel with 33 HAL peripherals, 41 product profiles, SMP/AMP multicore. Secure bootloader with A/B slots, Ed25519 signing, and measured boot.

Explore EoS
EAI + ENI
Intelligence

On-device LLM inference with 12 model families, ReAct agents, and LoRA fine-tuning. 1,024-channel neural interface for BCI, sEMG, and EEG applications.

Explore EAI
EIPC + eDB
Data & Comms

Capability-secured inter-process communication with HMAC-SHA256 and zero-copy shared memory. Multi-model database with SQL, document, and key-value stores.

Explore EIPC
eOffice + eApps
Applications

11-app office suite with real-time CRDT collaboration. 43 cross-platform apps in C+LVGL covering productivity, media, games, and connectivity.

Explore eOffice
EoStudio + EoSim
Developer Tools

Full IDE with board picker, HAL configurator, and AI tutor. Simulate firmware on 150 virtual platforms with QEMU, Renode, and HIL bridge.

Explore EoStudio
eBuild + eFlow
Build System

18-command cross-compilation CLI targeting ARM, x86, and RISC-V. Visual node editor for sensor pipelines and automation logic without writing C.

Explore eBuild

Industry Use Cases

EmbeddedOS products compose into complete solutions across eight major device categories.

Healthcare & Wearables

EoS runs on nRF5340 inside HEALTH-KEY ULTRA and HEALTH-BAND Neuro. ENI captures 4-channel EEG and sEMG. EAI classifies neurological patterns in real time. eDB stores encrypted biometric history. eServiceApps delivers the companion mobile app.

Aerospace & UAV

AeroSwift Personal runs EoS on STM32H7 with ARINC-429 HAL. eBoot provides cryptographically verified staged boot. EIPC routes sensor data between flight-computer cores. EoSim validates firmware against a digital twin before hardware deployment.

EoSeBootEIPCEoSim
Learn more

Automotive & ADAS

eRadar360 Aegis One fuses 4× 77 GHz FMCW radar, 8× cameras, and V2X on EoS. EAI runs the threat-detection neural network in real time. EIPC safely routes CAN FD data between AUTOSAR partitions. eBuild cross-compiles the full stack for NXP S32K344.

EoSEAIEIPCeBuild
Learn more

Industrial & IIoT

EoS targets IEC 61131-3 PLCs and Modbus gateways. eDB stores time-series sensor data with AES-256 encryption. EIPC bridges OT and IT networks securely. eFlow provides a visual node editor for industrial automation logic without writing C.

EoSeDBEIPCeFlow
Learn more

Robotics & Cobots

EoS SMP runs on Cortex-A72 with real-time servo control. ENI reads sEMG for human-robot collaboration. EAI runs SLAM and path-planning models on-device. EoStudio provides a visual robot configuration and simulation environment.

EoSENIEAIEoStudio
Learn more

Smart City & Infrastructure

EoS runs on smart meters, traffic controllers, and 5G gateways. eDB stores utility telemetry. EIPC routes data between city subsystems. eFlow visualises sensor pipelines. EoSim validates firmware for 150 virtual platforms before deployment.

EoSeDBEIPCeFlowEoSim
Learn more

Medical Devices

EoS targets IEC 60601-1 certified medical hardware. ENI captures 1,024-channel neural signals for BCI and neurofeedback. EAI classifies seizure patterns and motor imagery. eDB stores HIPAA-compliant patient data with full audit trails.

Energy & CleanTech

EoS runs on BMS controllers and solar inverters. eDB stores energy telemetry and grid state. EIPC routes data between battery packs and inverter controllers. EoSim validates firmware for energy hardware before deployment.

Open Source. Forever.

EmbeddedOS Research Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Every line of code is MIT-licensed. No vendor lock-in, no proprietary blobs, no closed APIs. We believe the infrastructure for intelligent devices should be a public good — freely available to every engineer, student, and researcher on Earth.
