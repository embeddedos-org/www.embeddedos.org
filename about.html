ABOUT US

Bringing Together the Global Ecosystem

We unite academia, industry, and government to advance education and engineering in embedded systems through EoS, EAI (AI Layer), ENI (Neural Interface Adapter), EIPC (Embedded IPC), and eBootloader—supporting innovation from startups to industry manufacturing across all versatile platforms.
