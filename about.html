Our Principles

Four pillars that guide every decision

Every line of code, every governance decision, and every partnership is evaluated against these four principles.

🔓
Open
All 14 components are Apache 2.0 licensed. No open-core, no enterprise tiers, no proprietary lock-in. The full stack is free for any use.
🏛
Foundation-Governed
A 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a Technical Steering Committee, transparent governance, and no single corporate controller. Decisions belong to the community.
👥
Member-Driven
Members vote on roadmap priorities, elect the TSC, and shape the technical direction. Individual contributors have the same voice as corporate sponsors.
🏭
Industry-Deployed
Built for production. Every component ships with CI-verified test coverage, release binaries, and a compatibility matrix. Not a research prototype.

History

From three components to a complete ecosystem

2022
Q1 2022
Foundation incorporated as 501(c)(3)
EmbeddedOS Research Foundation incorporated. Initial three components: EoS RTOS kernel, eBootloader, and eBuild toolchain. First GitHub organization created.
2022
Q3 2022
First sustaining members join
First corporate sustaining members join the foundation. Technical Steering Committee formed. Governance charter ratified by founding members.
2023
Q2 2023
EAI 0.5 — first on-device LLM inference
EAI 0.5 ships with INT8 quantized model inference on ARM Cortex-M7. First public demonstration of LLM inference on a microcontroller without cloud dependency.
2023
Q4 2023
ENI 0.4 — neural interface stack
ENI 0.4 ships with 64-channel spike detection and BLE streaming. First open-source neural interface RTOS stack. Collaboration with three university research groups announced.
2024
Q2 2024
eos-platform 1.0 — unified meta-distribution
eos-platform 1.0 ships as the first stable release covering all 14 components. Unified CI pipeline (eos-stack-manifest) goes live with 20 parallel build jobs.
2025
Q1 2025
EAI 0.9 — INT4 LLM, 11 tok/s on M85
EAI 0.9 ships with INT4 quantization and achieves 11 tokens/second on ARM Cortex-M85. 1.3B parameter model fits in 312 MB flash. World record for on-device LLM inference on a microcontroller.
2026
2026 Roadmap
ENI 1.0 (1,024 channels), Mobile apps, EoStudio 1.0
ENI 1.0 targeting 1,024-channel full-cortex recording. Mobile apps (iOS/Android) for HealthKey-Ulta, eBrowser, and eOffice. EoStudio 1.0 with formally verified RTOS debugger. Formal verification of EoS RTOS context-switch path.
