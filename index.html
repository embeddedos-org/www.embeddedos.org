Why EmbeddedOS

Built to be the best.
Not just another RTOS.

Every design decision in EmbeddedOS is made with one goal: to build the most capable, most complete, and most open embedded AI platform in the world.

🧠
First-class On-Device AI
EAI runs INT4-quantized LLMs at 11 tok/s on a 480 MHz microcontroller — no cloud, no latency, no privacy risk. No other embedded platform offers this level of on-device intelligence.
⚡
Deterministic Performance
EoS RTOS delivers sub-microsecond wake latency and formally verified context switching. ENI processes 1,024 neural channels in 800 µs — determinism you can stake a medical device on.
🔒
Security by Design
eBootloader implements a full chain-of-trust with hardware root-of-trust, A/B OTA, and rollback protection. eDB encrypts all data at rest. Security is not an afterthought — it is the foundation.
🌎
Full-Stack Coverage
From the first boot instruction to the AI agent on your phone — EmbeddedOS covers firmware, desktop, mobile (iOS/Android), browser extensions, and web. No other open-source platform spans this range.
🔬
Research-Grade Quality
100% test coverage target across all 18 repositories. Every component is built with the rigor of academic research and the reliability of production software. Peer-reviewed architecture decisions.
🤝
Open Foundation Governance
A 501(c)(3) nonprofit foundation — like Apache, Eclipse, or CNCF — ensures EmbeddedOS remains permanently open, community-governed, and free from vendor lock-in.
