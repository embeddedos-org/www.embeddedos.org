Vision

EmbeddedOS: The Operating System for Every Device.

"Every device that computes should have a secure, capable, open-source operating system — from the smallest microcontroller in a medical implant to the avionics computer in a VTOL aircraft."

— EmbeddedOS Research Foundation

The Problem

The embedded systems world is fragmented. Hundreds of proprietary RTOSes, incompatible HALs, vendor lock-in, and no common platform for AI, security, or connectivity. Developers spend months on boilerplate instead of building products.

The Solution

A coherent, open-source, vertically integrated stack: one kernel, one IPC layer, one AI runtime, one build system, one app store. Designed to work together from day one, on 52+ boards, from 64 KB microcontrollers to multi-core SoCs.

Five-Year Goals (2026–2031)

2026
AArch64 kernel port, RISC-V BSP, formally verified context-switch, eAI 1.0 with on-device LLM, HEALTH-KEY ULTRA general availability
2027
IEC 61508 SIL-2 certification path, AeroSwift Personal FAA Part 103 compliance, 100+ supported boards, 1000+ community contributors
2028
Space-grade EoS on CubeSat mission, closed-loop eNI BCI clinical trial, distributed EoS cluster for IoT edge computing
2030
EmbeddedOS running on 1 million devices worldwide, AeroSwift Transit urban air mobility certification, post-quantum cryptography standard
