Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The EmbeddedOS (EoS) Research Foundation collects minimal personal information. When you contact us, join as a member, or make a donation, we may collect your name, email address, and payment information. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to: respond to inquiries, process memberships and donations, send relevant updates about our projects, and improve our website and services.

3. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for essential functionality only. We use Google Fonts which may set cookies. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. If Sentry error monitoring is enabled, it may collect anonymous error data to improve site reliability.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services: Google Fonts (typography), Web3Forms (contact form), Stripe (payment processing), Sentry (error monitoring), GitHub (source code hosting). Each service has its own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@embeddedos.org.

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at privacy@embeddedos.org.
