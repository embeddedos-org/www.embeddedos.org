The Problem We're Solving

Proprietary embedded operating systems like VxWorks and QNX cost $50,000–$500,000 per deployment in licensing fees. This locks out:

  • 🎓 University researchers and students
  • 🌍 Engineers in developing nations
  • 🚀 Early-stage startups building hardware
  • 🏥 Non-profit medical device projects
  • 🛰 Open-source space and robotics projects

EmbeddedOS Foundation eliminates this barrier. Every product we build is free, open-source, and production-grade. Your donation makes this possible.
