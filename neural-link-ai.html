Neural Link AI

eNI — the EmbeddedOS Neural Interface platform for brain-computer interfaces and neural signal processing.

What is eNI?

eNI is the EmbeddedOS neural interface platform — a complete software stack for acquiring, processing, and acting on neural signals from brain-computer interface (BCI) hardware. It runs on ARM Cortex-M7 and supports up to 1024 simultaneous channels.

The 4-stage pipeline processes signals in real time: amplification → 24-bit ADC → digital filter bank → spike detector. Total pipeline latency: under 2ms on a 216 MHz Cortex-M7.

Key Specifications

Max Channels1024
ADC Resolution24-bit
Sample Rate30 kSps/ch
Pipeline Latency< 2ms
Target MCUCortex-M7 @216MHz
