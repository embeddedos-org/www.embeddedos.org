Code of Conduct

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the EmbeddedOS community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

  • Using welcoming and inclusive language
  • Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
  • Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
  • Focusing on what is best for the community
  • Showing empathy towards other community members

Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders at conduct@embeddedos.org. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
