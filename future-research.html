Future Research Directions

Long-term research goals and moonshot projects for the EmbeddedOS ecosystem.

🔬

Formal Verification

Formally verified context-switch path and memory safety proofs for the EoS kernel using Coq or Lean4. Target: IEC 61508 SIL-3 certification path for medical and aerospace applications.

🧬

Closed-Loop Neural Interfaces

Real-time closed-loop BCI systems: eNI reads neural signals, eAI classifies intent, EoS delivers stimulation feedback within 5ms. Applications: motor rehabilitation, prosthetics, and communication aids.

🌐

Distributed Embedded OS

eIPC extended to cross-device communication: a cluster of microcontrollers running EoS as a single distributed system with shared memory, message passing, and fault tolerance.

🚀

Space-Grade EoS

Radiation-hardened EoS port for CubeSat and small satellite missions. Collaboration with university space programs to fly EoS on orbit by 2028.

⚡

Sub-mW AI Inference

Ultra-low-power neural network inference on energy-harvesting devices. Target: run a keyword-spotting model on a device powered entirely by ambient RF or solar energy.

🔐

Post-Quantum Cryptography

Integrate NIST-standardized post-quantum algorithms (ML-KEM, ML-DSA) into eBoot and eIPC for quantum-resistant secure boot and inter-process communication.
