EmbeddedOS Health Devices

Patent-pending wearable health intelligence — four flagship products powered by EmbeddedOS on nRF52840, delivering clinical-grade biometrics in everyday form factors.

🔗 EoS Health Mono-Repo 📄 Patent Documentation 🔬 Research
🔑

HEALTH-KEY ULTRA

World's First USB-C Keychain Health Monitor · Patent Pending

The world's first keychain health monitor with a USB-C Male plug that connects directly to any device. ECG via USB-C shield conductor, SpO₂, BAC breath analysis, sEMG, TENS neuromodulation, 64GB onboard flash, and BLE 5.0 — all in a form factor smaller than a house key.

MCUnRF52840 + STM32
ConnectivityUSB-C + BLE 5.0
Storage64 GB Flash
BatteryLi-Po + USB-C charge
  • ECG (USB-C shield)
  • SpO₂
  • BAC Breath
  • sEMG
  • TENS
  • BLE 5.0
  • 64GB Flash

HEALTH-BAND Neuro

Neural Interface Wristband · Patent Pending

A next-generation neural wristband with 1,024-channel sEMG array, full-band EEG, ECG, PPG, SpO₂, galvanic skin response, and TENS neuromodulation. Designed for clinical neurology, BCI research, and consumer wellness.

MCUnRF52840
sEMG Channels1,024 array
ConnectivityBLE 5.0 + USB-C
BatteryWireless charging
  • 1024-ch sEMG
  • Full-band EEG
  • ECG
  • PPG
  • SpO₂
  • GSR
  • TENS
  • BLE 5.0

HEALTH-RING

Smart Health Ring · Continuous Biometric Monitoring

An ultra-compact smart ring delivering continuous 24/7 biometric monitoring — heart rate, SpO₂, skin temperature, sleep staging, activity tracking, and stress detection — all in a titanium ring form factor with 7-day battery life.

MCUnRF52840
MaterialTitanium shell
Battery7-day + wireless charge
Water ResistanceIP68 (100m)
  • PPG HR
  • SpO₂
  • Skin Temp
  • Sleep Staging
  • Activity
  • Stress
  • BLE 5.0
Mono-Repo GitHub Research

HEALTH-LAB

Portable Clinical Lab · Point-of-Care Diagnostics

A portable point-of-care diagnostic device delivering clinical-grade lab results in minutes. Blood glucose, HbA1c, lipid panel, complete blood count, electrolytes, and biomarker analysis — powered by EmbeddedOS with cloud sync and HIPAA-compliant data storage.

MCUnRF52840 + STM32H7
ConnectivityBLE 5.0 + WiFi + USB-C
Display2.4" color LCD
ComplianceHIPAA · ISO 13485
  • Blood Glucose
  • HbA1c
  • Lipid Panel
  • CBC
  • Electrolytes
  • Biomarkers
